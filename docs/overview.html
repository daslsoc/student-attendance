Student Attendance

Phone-friendly attendance and book-distribution tracking for the Dhamma & Sinhala School of Canberra. Teachers sign in with a magic link, tap who's present per subject and class, and reports roll it up by day and year. Paid students enrol automatically from student-registration.

Key features

✉️

Passwordless login

One-time email magic link; re-requesting re-sends the same link; session expires after 4 h.

👆

Tap-to-mark attendance

Pick subject + class, tap present students; sticky live count, search, select-all for phones.

📚

Book distribution

Same tap flow, recorded once per year per subject + class.

🗓️

Back-fill grid

Editable grid of students × dates for sessions that were missed.

📊

Reports

Today's per-class counts with drill-down; full-year sortable grid with per-student totals (DataTables).

🔄

Registration sync

Cron or Sync now pulls paid students; removed students are unenrolled but their history kept.

🔑

Roles & permissions

Administrator / Coordinator / Teacher; every route gated by a permission atom.

👩‍🏫

Teacher admin

Add teachers by name + email; removal deactivates and kills live links and sessions.

🧾

Audit log

Append-only record of every account and permission change, with before/after.

❓

Help guide

Screenshot-led help page, regenerated by a guarded Dusk capture.

🛠️

Recovery tools

MergeStudents, RecoverAttendanceFromLog and allocation export commands.

🧪

Prod-parity tests

Tests and Dusk run on MySQL even though local dev can use sqlite.

Access model

✅ How access works

  • Only people in users can request a login link
  • Students appear only in the (subject, class) they're enrolled in
  • Deactivated teachers lose links and sessions immediately
  • Removed students keep their attendance history

⛔ Watch

  • Security rests on teachers' email accounts (magic links)
  • Never delete the last account able to manage teachers
  • Don't edit enrolments by hand: registration is the source of truth
